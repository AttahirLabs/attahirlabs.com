What you can do

Every change has a story.

Find the change

Search and filter supported product, price, collection, and other configured store records. Tracking starts after setup and cannot recreate missing earlier history.

Understand the context

Inspect retained before-and-after values and flag matching changes with review rules. Optional Shopify-reported activity labels are not verified staff identities.

Assess selective restoration

Review the retained evidence and eligibility before explicitly confirming supported field restoration. Not every change can be reversed.

Configure wider coverage

Inventory, order summaries, pages, and themes require the relevant Shopify access, privacy admission, or configured observations. Delays and gaps remain visible.

Export retained history

Use formula-safe CSV exports or the read-only API. The API shares a limit of 100 requests per hour per installation.

Choose optional notifications

Configure supported monitoring, review rules, email, Slack, Discord, and summaries where available. Notifications require service setup and merchant opt-in.

History and restoration evidence

Keep the record. Check the evidence.

Ordinary history has no automatic age-based expiry while installed. Activity and restoration evidence are retained for up to 120 days. Shorter merchant settings, uninstall cleanup, and privacy erasure take precedence. An ordinary history entry can remain after its restoration evidence expires; the app cannot recover previously deleted records.

Read the StoreChronicle privacy disclosure for deletion, backup, and external-recipient handling.

Common questions

Before you install StoreChronicle.

Is StoreChronicle available on the Shopify App Store?

Yes. Install StoreChronicle from its Shopify App Store listing. All available supported features are free.

What does Free include?

All available supported features, including store history, review rules, CSV export, a read-only API, selective restoration, configured content monitoring, and optional notifications. Required access, privacy setup, service configuration, opt-in, and staff confirmations still apply.

Does StoreChronicle identify exactly who made a change?

No. It can show optional Shopify-reported activity labels where available. These labels are not verified staff identities, and related activity is not proof of who caused a specific recorded change. Unknown attribution stays unknown.

Can I roll back my entire Shopify store?

No. StoreChronicle is not a whole-store backup or disaster-recovery service. Selective restoration is limited to eligible retained fields, requires Shopify permissions and explicit confirmation, and cannot reverse every downstream effect. Order and theme restoration are not offered.

Does it record every change in real time?

No. Supported Shopify events and configured observations provide evidence, but delays, outages, access restrictions, and observation intervals can leave gaps. Tracking starts after setup; missing history is not presented as complete.

How long is history retained?

Ordinary history has no automatic age-based expiry while installed. Activity and restoration evidence are retained for up to 120 days. Shorter store settings, uninstall cleanup, and privacy erasure take precedence; expired evidence cannot be restored.

Can I export my history?

Free includes formula-safe CSV export and a read-only API with a shared limit of 100 requests per hour per installation. Exports cover available retained records; a multi-part export is not an atomic whole-store snapshot.

What happens when I uninstall?

Authenticated Shopify uninstall and shop-deletion notifications trigger store-data cleanup. Reinstalling does not guarantee recovery of deleted history. See the StoreChronicle privacy disclosure for backup and external-recipient handling.
