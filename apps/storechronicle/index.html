Supported scope

Know what your history includes.

Coverage depends on your plan, Shopify permissions, enabled observations, and retained evidence. Tracking starts after the relevant setup; it cannot recreate an earlier history Shopify did not provide.

Current feature coverage by plan
Store dataPlanWhat to expect
Products and pricesFree, Standard, ProSupported product and variant field changes with available before-and-after values.
Collections and inventoryStandard, ProSupported collection fields, membership observations, and inventory quantities. Inventory requires additional access.
OrdersProA limited order summary, not the full order or customer profile. Requires access and privacy setup.
Pages and themesProConfigured observations of supported page and theme content. Observations do not capture every intervening edit.
Review rules and notificationsStandard, ProRules flag matching changes for review. Optional email, Slack, Discord, and weekly summaries require setup and opt-in.
CSV export and read-only APIProExport retained history or read it through the authenticated API, subject to the app’s limits.

Restoration is selective and evidence-dependent: supported product, variant, collection, page, or inventory fields may be eligible. Order and theme restoration are not offered. StoreChronicle is not a whole-store backup.

Two different retention periods

Change history is not the same as restoration evidence.

Shorter store settings and privacy deletion still apply. Evidence can expire while an ordinary change-history entry remains.

Common questions

Before you use StoreChronicle.

Does StoreChronicle identify exactly who made a change?

No. It can show optional Shopify-reported activity labels where available. These labels are not verified staff identities, and related activity is not proof of who caused a specific recorded change. Unknown attribution stays unknown.

Can I roll back my entire Shopify store?

No. StoreChronicle is not a whole-store backup or disaster-recovery service. Selective restoration is limited to eligible retained fields, requires Shopify permissions and confirmation, and cannot reverse every downstream effect. Other apps or staff can make concurrent edits.

Does it record every change in real time?

No. Supported Shopify events and configured observations provide evidence, but delayed delivery, outages, access restrictions, and observation intervals can leave gaps. The app shows recovery and unresolved-gap information instead of claiming missing history is complete.

Can I export my history?

Pro includes formula-safe CSV export and a read-only API. Exports cover available, entitled records; multi-part exports are not an atomic snapshot of the entire store. A confirmed downgrade also provides a seven-day read-only ordinary-history export grace.

What happens when I uninstall?

Authenticated Shopify uninstall and shop-deletion notifications trigger store-data cleanup. Reinstalling does not guarantee recovery of deleted history. Backup copies and external recipients have separate handling, explained in the StoreChronicle privacy disclosure.

Is StoreChronicle available on the Shopify App Store?

Not yet. The app is in development-store validation and its App Store listing is being prepared. Contact support@attahirlabs.com with questions. This page does not represent Shopify approval.
