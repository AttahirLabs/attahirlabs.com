What you can do

From stock review to a confirmed clearance plan.

Find the SKUs to review

Scan inventory and available sales history. Review saved scan progress, freshness and cost coverage, then select individual sizes or colors across matching results.

Set a staged markdown plan

Follow guided stock selection, enter your shop-zone schedule, and review original and target prices. Choose stages, exclusions, price floors and discount caps; optional gross-margin rules require verified costs in the selected currency.

Choose the right price scope

Manage base prices or one supported geographic market catalog. Country prices and compare-at values must agree for a shared catalog markdown.

Control how a campaign ends

Pause future changes, end and restore saved originals, or end and keep verified markdowns. Outside edits are flagged for review.

Prepare clearance merchandising

Apply campaign tags, prepare a selected manual clearance collection, and connect campaign events to Shopify Flow. Product-level actions can affect all variants.

Review results and prepare a handoff

Inspect allocated campaign sales, refunds and net units. Export audits, outcomes, recovery records and marketing briefs with current stock and price checks.

Common questions

Before you install StockClearance.

What is StockClearance?

StockClearance by Attahir Labs is an inventory clearance app for Shopify. It helps merchants review dead stock and slow-moving inventory, select variants, preview staged markdowns, and manage clearance campaigns.

Can I start for free?

Yes. All current StockClearance features are included with no app subscription charge or product-count plan limit. Campaigns support up to 2,048 selected variants; large catalogs require a separate capacity check.

Can I clear selected sizes or colors?

Yes. Choose individual variants or all variants matching your filters. Product tags and collections still apply at product level.

How do staged markdowns work?

Each stage uses the saved original price, with your floor and discount cap. The final stage holds its price; an end date or end action controls restoration.

Can I schedule a clearance campaign?

Yes. The guided workflow lets you select stock, enter a schedule in your shop’s time zone, and review prices before confirming. Saved future campaigns can be revised or ended before they start.

Can it protect my margin?

Optional minimum gross-margin rules require a verified cost in the selected currency. The calculation excludes tax, shipping, fees, overhead and other discounts, so it does not guarantee final order profitability.

Which market catalogs are supported?

Base prices and supported geographic market catalogs can be managed separately. A catalog supports up to eight market contexts and eight active countries with matching effective prices and compare-at values. Competing catalogs, B2B company-location and retail contexts are blocked. Cross-currency inherited pricing requires Markets and publication read access; shop-currency costs remain unknown in another currency.

What happens when I stop a campaign?

Pause keeps prices and stops future changes. End and restore restores matching saved app-owned originals. End and keep retains verified markdowns and ends progression. Outside edits and uncertain operations require review.

Does it overwrite compare-at prices?

The default preserves existing compare-at prices. An optional policy fills an empty compare-at price with the variant’s original price. Storefront display depends on your theme, selected variant and market.

Can I reuse a clearance policy?

Yes. Save and copy policies, or import a template for another shop. Templates contain configuration, stay inactive, and require a fresh stock selection and price review.

How do I follow scan progress?

The workspace shows saved waiting, running, retry and failure states. Review completed catalog observations before selecting stock. Unavailable sales history stays labelled unavailable.

Does it create bundle products?

Curated offers create quantity discounts on 2–5 selected variants. Repeated units of one eligible SKU can qualify. They do not create a new bundle product or require one of every item.

What do campaign outcomes measure?

Outcomes allocate observed order lines to campaign item windows and subtract refunded line quantities. They show coverage gaps and do not prove extra revenue caused by the app or physical returns. Gross-profit calculations require verified costs.

Does it send customer promotions?

Marketing briefs prepare editable draft copy after checking stock and prices. Exports and Flow events do not themselves send customer marketing or buy ads.

What data does StockClearance use?

Product, variant, inventory, price, available cost, market/catalog context, discount and campaign records, and order-line signals support the workflow. See the StockClearance privacy policy section.

How do I stop using StockClearance?

Review and end active campaigns, recover pending operations, and deactivate offers before uninstalling. Free access has no app subscription to cancel. Uninstalling removes the access needed for further price restoration.
