TariffShield

Review duties and margins for U.S. quartz surface products.

Scan your Shopify catalog, enter reviewed filing facts, and see duties and margins for supported U.S. quartz surface product entries. Unknown products show what needs review. Compare scenarios, receive opted-in summaries, and confirm price updates using the exact amounts displayed.

Current coverage: ordinary General U.S. entries under HTSUS 6810990020, 6810990040 and 7020006000, from supported origins. Product scope, manufacturing origin, AD/CVD treatment and quota disposition must be reviewed. Unsupported or incomplete inputs produce no estimate.

Common questions

Before you install TariffShield.

Can I calculate duties or export results?

Supported current U.S. QSP calculations are available while signed evidence is current. Pro includes current catalog CSV export. Unsupported and incomplete products have no duty or margin estimate.

Will my prices change automatically?

No. Every price update requires explicit confirmation of the displayed variant and amounts. Bulk confirmation is available on Standard and Pro.

What products and markets are supported?

Ordinary General U.S. entries under HTSUS 6810990020, 6810990040 and 7020006000, with supported origins and complete reviewed declarations. Canada, China, Russia, India, Türkiye, Nicaragua and Malaysia are outside this authority slice. Broad multi-market analysis remains unavailable.

Does the app classify my products or verify broker documents?

No. You supply reviewed classification, Note 41(a) product scope, original slab provenance, customs value, AD/CVD determination and quota evidence where applicable. The app checks whether those declarations match signed authority.

Are these final customs charges?

No. Results are planning estimates for the exact supplied facts, not customs classification, liquidation or legal determinations. Brokerage, taxes, processing fees and unsupported programs are excluded.

What happens when evidence expires?

Current numeric guidance stops until a reviewed release is active. Historical records remain labeled separately.

What are the current billing options?

The Shopify listing offers Free, Standard at $19 per month, and Pro at $190 per year. The annual option is listed as Pro. Review the features and subscription terms on Shopify before approving a plan.

Does TariffShield offer a paid-plan trial?

The current listing offers a free plan for 10 SKUs and does not advertise a paid-plan free trial.

What data does TariffShield use?

The app uses product, variant, inventory, price, available cost, origin, classification, and configuration data, along with saved analysis and price-action records. See the TariffShield section of our privacy policy.
