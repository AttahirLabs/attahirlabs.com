Adaptations for this local prototype

The characters are modified asset copies used in an Unreal cafe prototype. Their deformation skeletons were reorganized into anatomical hierarchies with global bind poses preserved, and an unweighted identity root was added for retargeting. Local cast instances use retargeted motion, posing, scale and material/wardrobe variations. These changes do not alter the downloaded original blend files.

Snow and Rain: supplied image textures adapted to standard PBR. Body skin UDIM tiles explicitly split into ordinary per-tile materials and UV offsets to preserve complete face/body geometry. Clothing, hair, eyes and original skin weights retained. Snow's tooth/gum surfaces use documented rigid tooth-centre bindings; custom Blender shaders, rig UI, facial controls and corrective/lattice behavior are not reproduced.

Rex: original authored modeled, weighted hair replaces legacy script-driven particle fibres. Original procedural clothing base colors were baked offline into textures, retaining the authored knit pattern and trim. Fine fibres, advanced bump/displacement/roughness/anisotropy and facial corrective/lattice controls are simplified or omitted. Tooth/gum surfaces use rigid head/jaw bindings in place of SurfaceDeform. Eleven verified unweighted DCC helper joints were removed from the skin; positive-weight bone bindings and weight values are preserved. The publisher supports the original rig in Blender 3.3–3.6; the separately audited asset-only conversion used Blender 5.2 without executing embedded source scripts.

Detailed source provenance, conversion changes and validation reports remain beside each model in SourceAssets/Snow, SourceAssets/Rain and SourceAssets/Rex. Rendered appearance, deformation and contact quality are separate Unreal validation results.
