# Café motion study

This is a review-only homepage animation pilot. It is not linked from the public site and is marked `noindex,nofollow`.

The three original image-generation keyframes were created in `photorealistic-natural` mode at 1536 × 1024, then saved as optimized JPGs in `assets/experiments/cafe-motion`. The MP4 uses optical-flow interpolation at 24 fps to stage a barista-to-customer cup handoff. It is an animatic, not a rigged or physically rendered character performance. It should not replace the current homepage hero until the store design and acting are approved and production motion is made.

Prompt direction: an original cinematic miniature café, complete independent cutaway shop from floor to ceiling, eight to ten proportionately scaled adult workers and customers, natural anatomy and clothing, a barista serving a customer, other patrons sitting and browsing, believable separation from tables and equipment, warm filmic light, real wood/ceramic/steel/fabric materials, elevated three-quarter camera, clean backdrop, restrained Attahir Labs palette. Subsequent prompts preserved camera, architecture, cast, and wardrobe while advancing the cup handoff and exit.
